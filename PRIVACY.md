# CLO-V Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

CLO-V helps people share outfit photos, vote on Fit-Checks, and organize a personal wardrobe. This policy explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

## Information we store

- **Account information:** your email address and an account identifier provided by our authentication service.
- **Profile information:** your nickname, avatar, and style preferences.
- **Photos:** Fit-Check, avatar, and wardrobe photos that you choose to upload.
- **Try-on photos:** If you choose virtual try-on, the full-body photo you select and the selected wardrobe photos are sent to WEARFITS to generate a preview image.
- **App activity:** votes, short comments, friendships, notifications, Fit-Checks, and wardrobe categories needed to provide the app's features.

## How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, display your profile and posts, deliver voting and social features, secure the service, and improve the app's reliability. We do not sell personal information or use uploaded photos for advertising.

## Storage and sharing

App data is stored using Supabase services. Photos are stored in private storage and delivered through temporary secure links. Information is shared with other CLO-V users only when the feature you use requires it, such as showing a Fit-Check in the feed or showing your nickname beside a vote.

Virtual try-on is optional. When you tap **Try on**, CLO-V sends your selected full-body photo and selected wardrobe clothing photos to WEARFITS through CLO-V's server. WEARFITS generates a still-image preview. Its [current privacy policy](https://wearfits.com/privacy) says generative try-on input images are deleted within 24 hours and generated results may be retained for up to 30 days. WEARFITS states that user images and personal data are not used to train AI models without notifying users. Check the linked policy for current details. CLO-V does not save the full-body input photo; it remains in app memory for the request.

## Your controls

You can edit your profile, delete Fit-Checks, remove wardrobe items, and permanently delete your account from the Account screen. Permanent account deletion removes your profile and the CLO-V content associated with it.

You can choose whether to grant photo-library access. The app only receives photos that you select through the system photo picker.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the CLO-V team through the project repository: <https://github.com/rhdmsrb/Clo-v/issues>.
